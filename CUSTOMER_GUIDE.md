# Your Blog Approval Dashboard — User Guide

Welcome! This is your control center for approving blog posts before they go live on your website.

## Getting Started

### Step 1: Bookmark Your Dashboard

You have a unique dashboard link. Save it in your browser:

```
https://blog-dashboard.vercel.app/?client=your-business-name
```

### Step 2: Check It Weekly

You'll receive an email notification when a new blog is ready for review. Click the link in the email or visit your dashboard.

### Step 3: Approve or Request Changes

See the instructions below.

---

## How to Use Your Dashboard

### Dashboard Layout

Your dashboard has 3 sections:

1. **Left side: Blogs Waiting for Approval** ⏳
   - Shows blogs generated and ready for your review
   - Click any blog to see a preview

2. **Right side: Blog Preview** 📋
   - Click a blog on the left to see the full preview
   - Shows the title, content, and hero image
   - This is exactly how it will appear on your website

3. **Bottom: Published Blogs** ✅
   - Shows blogs you've already approved
   - Your publishing history

---

## Approving a Blog

### Option 1: Approve & Publish (Recommended)

1. **Click on a blog** from the "Waiting for Approval" section
2. **Review the content** and image in the preview pane
3. **Click "✅ Approve & Publish"**
4. **Done!** The blog is now live on your website

Takes 10 seconds. That's it.

### Option 2: Request Changes

If you want to edit something:

1. **Click on the blog** to preview it
2. **Click "📝 Request Changes"**
3. **We'll revise it** and resubmit for approval next week
4. You'll get another notification to review the updated version

---

## What to Look For When Approving

### ✅ Good to Approve
- Title is clear and matches your business
- Content is relevant and well-written
- Image looks professional
- No typos or grammatical errors
- Tone matches your brand voice

### ❌ Request Changes If
- Title is unclear or off-topic
- Content has errors
- Image doesn't match the topic
- Tone feels wrong for your brand
- Anything feels incomplete

---

## Tips & Best Practices

### Batch Approval
- Check your dashboard **once a week** (saves time!)
- Approve all pending blogs at once
- It only takes a few minutes

### Regular Submissions
- The more blog topics you submit, the more content you get
- Submit topics weekly = 4 new blogs per month
- Submit daily = 30 new blogs per month

### Review Frequency
- Check your email for notifications
- Or check dashboard daily (takes 30 seconds)
- Set a calendar reminder if you prefer structured time

## FAQ

**Q: Do I need to know how to code?**  
A: No. Just click "Approve" or "Request Changes." That's all.

**Q: What if I don't approve a blog?**  
A: No problem. Click "Request Changes" and we'll revise it. Nothing publishes without your approval.

**Q: Can I edit the blog myself?**  
A: Not directly in the dashboard. Tell us your edits when you request changes, and we'll make them.

**Q: How long does a revised blog take?**  
A: We revise it and resubmit within 24 hours.

**Q: Where do approved blogs appear?**  
A: On your website's blog page, automatically.

**Q: Can I schedule blogs for later?**  
A: Let us know. We can schedule them to publish on specific dates.

**Q: What if I don't like a blog's topic?**  
A: Tell us the topics you prefer, and we'll focus on those instead.
